Four ways to build mySleep

"Tonight looks empty" and "I'm bombarded instead of just going to sleep" pull in opposite directions. What settles it is what the app shows when you open it, and where your past nights live. Here are four structures, each shown at the moment it matters most, with what is good and bad about it. My pick is at the bottom. Same myGym look throughout, example data.

My pickone home that changes with the time of day

Like myGym's Home: one full page. In the evening the top is about tonight, in the morning it is last night's report; your week and the trend sit underneath either way. Sleep is the round button in the middle.

My pick: A, with one thing from C

A answers both of your complaints: home is never empty, yet at bedtime the first thing is tonight and the round Sleep button, so you are never reading your way to bed. It is the structure myGym already has, so it will feel familiar. From C I would borrow the coloured month calendar as the top of the Journal tab, instead of a plain list.

B is the honest alternative if you want bedtime to be a single purposeful screen and are happy to go to the Journal for data. C and D each go too far in one direction.
